Scope: the single app route (`app/page.tsx`) plus the 3D scene (`components/office/*`). Visitor mode: Operate, demo-ready for a 3-minute jury pitch.

Audience and job: the encargado de zona sees which city needs attention, reads the three crossed signals, and approves or rejects one concrete order. The jury must grasp the mechanism within one viewport.

Constraints: Farmaenlace palette pinned (navy #001A8C, lime #7DBA00, gray #868686, corporate blue #08467F). All data synthetic and labeled. Convex `board`, `ensureScenario`, `decide` stay as they are.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The recommendation is a dispatch order from the distribution center, not a dashboard card. Refuses the SaaS sidebar + KPI tiles and the cream editorial page.

OWN-WORLD: White label stock, navy ink, 1px hairline-ruled fields, condensed Archivo numerals, generated barcode strips, zero radius. Lime is reserved for the action layer only (proposed, released). Every artifact carries a code: ORD-GYE-0142, WA/INV/PRO, GYE/UIO/CUE. States are printed marks: pending dashed, released lime stamp, rejected struck through.

STORY: Guayaquil is flagged; three agents show why (WhatsApp spike, shelf at 18%, no promo); the manager signs the order; the branch visibly restocks and the SmartClub hanger lights.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top route bar with wordmark and city route tabs. Left ~58%: isometric 3D branch diorama on corporate blue with three agents at their posts. Right: the dispatch label at full height, signal rows with data bars, order fields, approve/reject at the bottom of the label.

FORM: Dispatch order / logistics label, position 5 of 7, seed key c85df0e1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: signing the order stamps the label LIBERADA in lime, a pallet drops into the 3D branch, the Vitamina C shelf fills from 18% to 78%, and the SmartClub hanger switches on. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, one orchestrated release moment, reduced-motion skips the drop and shows the end state.
